📘 Data Dictionary

This document describes the dataset used in the Food Trends: Understanding Customer Preferences in Food & Beverages project.

📂 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains information related to restaurants, customer ratings, pricing patterns, cuisine preferences, delivery performance, geographic distribution, and customer sentiment. It is used to analyze food trends, customer behavior, operational efficiency, and market performance through interactive Power BI dashboards.

🧾 Column Descriptions

ID: Unique identifier assigned to each restaurant
Restaurant Names: Name of the restaurant
Address: Complete address of the restaurant
Area: Locality or neighborhood where the restaurant is located
City: City in which the restaurant operates
Cuisine: Type of cuisine served by the restaurant
Most Preferred: Indicates whether the cuisine is among the most preferred food categories
Price: Average cost level of the restaurant
Price Segment: Classification of restaurants into Budget, Mid-Range, or Premium categories
Most Preferred Price Segment: Preferred pricing category based on customer demand and popularity
Average Ratings: Average customer rating received by the restaurant
Total Ratings: Total number of customer ratings and reviews received
Total Orders: Total number of orders placed at the restaurant
Rating Segment: Classification of restaurants based on rating performance
Average Order Value: Average monetary value of each customer order
Order Frequency: Number of times customers place orders from the restaurant
Delivery Time: Average time taken to deliver customer orders
Delivery Segment: Categorization of delivery performance as Fast, Moderate, or Slow
Delivery Target: Target delivery time set for performance evaluation
Fast Delivery Orders: Number of orders delivered within the target delivery time
Fast Delivery %: Percentage of orders delivered within the fast delivery threshold
Sentiment: Overall customer sentiment derived from ratings and reviews
Top Performing City: City identified as having the highest restaurant performance metrics
Revenue: Total revenue generated by the restaurant
Customer Satisfaction Level: Classification of customer satisfaction based on ratings and sentiment
Restaurant Popularity Level: Popularity category determined by ratings, orders, and customer engagement
Restaurant Ranking Score: Composite score used to rank restaurant performance
User Engagement Level: Classification of customer interaction and engagement with the restaurant

📊 Notes

- Price segments are derived from restaurant pricing levels.
- Rating segments are based on average rating thresholds.
- Delivery segments are classified using delivery time ranges.
- Sentiment categories are generated through customer review and rating analysis.
- Several KPIs and business metrics are calculated using DAX measures in Power BI.
