📘 Data Dictionary

This document describes the dataset used in the Food Trends: Understanding Customer Preferences in Food & Beverages project.

📂 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains information about restaurants, customer ratings, pricing, delivery performance, and sentiment analysis.

🧾 Column Descriptions

ID: Unique identifier for each restaurant
Restaurant Name: Name of the restaurant
Address: Full address/location of the restaurant
Area: Specific locality or region within the city
City: City where the restaurant is located
Cuisine Type: Type of cuisine served (e.g., Indian, Chinese, etc.)
Most Preferred: Indicates whether the cuisine is highly preferred by customers
Price: Cost level of the restaurant
Price Segment: Category of pricing such as Budget, Mid-range, or Premium
Average Rating: Average customer rating given to the restaurant
Total Ratings: Total number of ratings received
Rating Segment: Categorization of ratings (Low, Moderate, High)
Delivery Time: Time taken by the restaurant to deliver orders
Delivery Segment: Category of delivery speed (Fast, Moderate, Slow)
Restaurant Sentiment: Overall sentiment derived from customer reviews (Positive, Neutral, Negative)

📊 Notes

- Price segments are derived based on pricing ranges
- Rating segments are categorized based on average rating thresholds
- Sentiment analysis is performed using customer feedback
